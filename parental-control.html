How to set it up

Ready for study time in four steps

No technical setup, no parent portal, no account. Install it on your child's Windows PC and it's ready in a couple of minutes.

1

Download and install

Grab the free installer, run it on the Windows 10 or 11 PC your child uses, and open the app. No account or sign-in needed.

2

Add what to block

List the websites and desktop apps that derail homework — YouTube, TikTok, Discord, games, whatever pulls their attention.

3

Set the rhythm and start

Choose the study length, break length, and how many cycles. Press Start and the blocked list goes dark immediately.

4

It holds until it's done

The session can't be paused or closed. Blocks lift automatically during breaks and when the session ends — or if the PC is fully restarted.

On the moment you start it. On until a restart.

There's no timer to babysit and no hidden off switch. From the second a focus session begins, every app and site on the list is enforced — for the full session, every session. It keeps working whether you're standing over their shoulder or in another room. The only thing that lifts the block early is a complete PC restart, and that's a deliberate, obvious act — not a quiet tap of a pause button.

During scheduled breaks everything unlocks — then the next study block locks it all again.

FAQ

Parent questions, answered

Is the parental control app really free?
Yes — completely free for now, with every feature included. There's no subscription, no per-child fee, no trial period, and no credit card required.
Can my child just turn it off?
No. While a focus session is running, the blocker can't be stopped, closed, paused, or uninstalled, and there's no settings back door. The only way to end a session early is to restart the whole PC — obvious, disruptive, and easy for you to notice.
Does it work on Windows 10 and Windows 11?
Yes. Distraction Blocker runs on both Windows 10 and Windows 11. Download the installer, run it, and it's ready — no configuration needed. The installer is scanned clean on VirusTotal; you can check the full report here.
Does it track or spy on my child?
No. There's no account, no analytics inside the app, and nothing leaves the computer. The block lists and timer settings stay on your PC. It's a focus tool, not a surveillance tool.
What if we need to stop a session early?
Restart the PC. That's the only way — and it's intentional. The friction of a full restart is what stops a session from being casually skipped. You can also stop the app freely during any scheduled break.
Can I use it for my own focus too?
Absolutely. Distraction Blocker started as a focus app for adults — the same Pomodoro sessions and blocking work just as well for your own deep work as they do for a child's study time. See the main focus-app page →
