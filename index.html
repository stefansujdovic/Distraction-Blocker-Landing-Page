How it works

Four steps between you and deep work

Built on the Pomodoro technique: focused sprints, short breaks, repeat. You set the rhythm once — the app enforces it.

1

Set your rhythm

Choose focus length, break length, and how many cycles to run. Set cycles to 0 for an infinite session — if you dare.

2

Pick your poison

Add the websites and desktop apps that steal your time — YouTube, Instagram, Slack, games, anything.

3

Hit Start

The timer begins and everything on your list goes dark. Blocked sites won't load, blocked apps won't open. No exceptions, no pause button.

4

Get notified, take a break

When a focus session ends, a notification tells you break time has started — apps and sites unlock so you can breathe. Then the next cycle begins.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What if I really need to stop a focus session?
Restart your PC. That's the only way — and it's intentional. The friction of a full restart is what makes the commitment real. You can also stop the app freely during any break.
Is it really free?
Yes — completely free for now, with every feature included. No trial period, no account, no payment details.
How do I know when a session ends?
You get a Windows notification the moment a focus session finishes, letting you know the break has started and your apps and websites are usable again. Another cycle begins automatically after the break.
Which versions of Windows are supported?
Windows 10 and Windows 11. Download the installer, run it, and you're set — no configuration needed.
Does it track what I do?
No. There's no account, no analytics, and nothing leaves your machine. Your block lists and timer settings stay on your PC.
