Version 1.0.0

Released July 7, 2026

The first public release.

  • Pomodoro cycles with your own focus and break lengths — a fixed number of rounds or infinite.
  • Blocks websites and desktop apps from separate lists.
  • No escape during focus — a running session can't be stopped, paused, or uninstalled until you restart your PC.
  • Windows notification the moment a focus session ends.
  • Real breaks — everything unlocks automatically between sessions.
  • No account, no sign-in, and no data leaving your PC.
